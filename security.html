Malware and Cyber Threats

WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, the WannaCry ransomware attack spread across computers in many countries, encrypting files and demanding payment to restore access. The attack disrupted hospitals, businesses, and other organizations, including the UK's National Health Service. This incident showed how unpatched software can expose computer systems to serious security threats and why organizations need regular updates, backups, and security planning. Its impact demonstrated the importance of cybersecurity. May 12, 2017

Common Cybersecurity Threats

  1. Ransomware attacks that lock files and demand payment.
  2. Phishing emails designed to steal personal information.

Ways to Protect Information

  1. Use strong, unique passwords and enable multifactor authentication.
  2. Install software updates to fix security vulnerabilities.

Cybersecurity Best Practices

  • Never click suspicious links or download unknown attachments.
  • Use antivirus software and keep it updated.
