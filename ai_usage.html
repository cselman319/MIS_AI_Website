Correct AI Usage

AI can be a helpful tool for college students when it is used responsibly. Students can use AI to brainstorm ideas, clarify difficult concepts, and improve their writing. However, AI should support learning rather than replace a student's own effort. Students should verify information, protect private information, and follow their instructors' rules.

AI critique: I agree that AI should support learning rather than replace it. Students benefit most when they use AI to ask questions, explore ideas, and check their understanding. They should also verify AI-generated information because it can contain errors. Following course policies and disclosing AI assistance helps maintain academic integrity.

Benefits of AI

  1. Helps students brainstorm ideas and understand difficult concepts.
  2. Provides feedback and helps improve writing.

Who Benefits?

  1. Students who use AI to support learning.
  2. Teachers and organizations that use AI responsibly.

Incorrect AI Usage

Incorrect AI use can undermine academic integrity and responsible decision-making. Students should not submit prohibited AI-generated work as their own or rely on unverified answers. AI should be used honestly, with attention to privacy, accuracy, and course rules.

Examples of Incorrect AI Use

Plagiarism
Submitting AI-generated work as your own when course rules prohibit it.

False Information
Using AI-generated facts or sources without checking their accuracy.

Privacy Violations
Sharing sensitive personal or confidential information with AI tools without permission.

Students should use AI responsibly and honestly in college. They should verify information before including it in assignments. They should follow their instructors' policies and protect private information. Responsible choices help preserve academic integrity and support genuine learning.
